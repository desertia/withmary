Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 15, 2026

1. Privacy Policy

With Mary (the “App”) values users’ personal information and processes personal information only to the extent necessary, in compliance with applicable laws and regulations. This Privacy Policy explains the types of personal information collected by the App, the purposes for which it is used, and the methods of retention and deletion.

2. Personal Information We Collect

The App may collect or store the following information in the course of providing the service.

  • Email address
  • User name
  • Profile photo, if provided by the user
  • Prayer history and data saved by the user in the App

3. Purposes of Using Personal Information

Personal information collected is used for the following purposes.

  • Account registration and login authentication
  • Saving prayer history and synchronizing it across multiple devices
  • Profile management
  • Providing and maintaining the service
  • Responding to errors and improving the service
  • Handling customer inquiries

4. Use of External Services

The App uses the following Google Firebase services to provide the service.

  • Firebase Authentication
  • Cloud Firestore
  • Firebase Storage

These services may process user information to the extent necessary to provide the service, and further details are governed by Google’s Privacy Policy.

5. Public Content

The App may display liturgical and faith information made publicly available by the Catholic Bishops' Conference of Korea. Copyright in such content remains with the original rights holders, and the App uses it for the purpose of providing relevant information to users.

6. Retention and Deletion of Personal Information

Personal information is retained for as long as necessary to provide the service. If a user deletes an account within the App, personal information and saved data associated with the account are processed in accordance with the deletion procedure. However, information that must be retained under applicable laws and regulations may be securely retained for the required period.

7. User Rights

Users may delete their accounts within the App at any time. Users may also make inquiries regarding access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal information.

8. Protection of Personal Information

The App applies reasonable technical and administrative safeguards to prevent the loss, theft, unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure of personal information.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be revised due to changes in applicable laws and regulations or in the service, and changes will be announced through this page.
