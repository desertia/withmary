👑 Glorious Mysteries

GLORIOUS MYSTERIES

The Glorious Mysteries contemplate the Resurrection and Ascension of Jesus, the descent of the Holy Spirit, and the glory of Mary.
They lead us to look toward the eternal life promised by God in the hope of the Resurrection.

FIRST MYSTERY

The Resurrection

Luke 24:1–8

We contemplate Jesus rising in victory over death and opening for us the hope of eternal life.

SECOND MYSTERY

The Ascension

Acts 1:6–11

We contemplate the risen Jesus entrusting his disciples with their mission and ascending into heaven.

THIRD MYSTERY

The Descent of the Holy Spirit

Acts 2:1–4

We contemplate Jesus sending the promised Holy Spirit and giving the disciples strength and courage to proclaim the Gospel.

FOURTH MYSTERY

The Assumption of Mary

Sacred Tradition

We contemplate the mystery of the Blessed Virgin Mary sharing in the glory of heaven after completing her earthly life.

FIFTH MYSTERY

The Coronation of Mary

Revelation 12:1

We contemplate the Blessed Virgin Mary, faithful to God, being glorified as Queen of heaven and earth.
