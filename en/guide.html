ROSARY GUIDE

How to Pray the Rosary

There is no need to rush.
With Mary, continue your prayer peacefully, one decade at a time.

What Is the Rosary?

The Rosary is a cherished Catholic prayer in which we meditate on the life of Jesus Christ in the company of the Blessed Virgin Mary. What matters most is gathering your heart in prayer, even for a few quiet moments each day.

How to Pray the Rosary

  1. Make the Sign of the Cross.
  2. Pray the Apostles’ Creed.
  3. Pray the Our Father.
  4. Pray three Hail Marys.
  5. Pray the Glory Be.
  6. Choose the Joyful, Luminous, Sorrowful, or Glorious Mysteries and pray the five decades.
  7. Pray the Hail, Holy Queen.
  8. Conclude with the closing prayer provided in the app.

If You Are New to the Rosary

You do not need to memorize every step before you begin. With Mary guides you naturally through the order of the prayers, so you can give your attention to prayer itself.

PRAYING MORE DEEPLY

How to Pray the Rosary More Deeply

Perfect concentration is not the goal. What matters is gently returning your wandering heart to prayer.

While praying the Rosary, your thoughts may wander, it may be difficult to focus, or the prayer may sometimes feel tedious. St. Louis de Montfort encourages us not to abandon the Rosary in these moments. What matters is the faithfulness to return to the mystery and the prayer whenever distractions arise.

01

Begin with a moment of stillness

Before beginning at once, take a moment to quiet your heart. Ask for the help of the Holy Spirit, rest in God’s presence, and then begin slowly.

02

Pause before each mystery

Before each decade, pause and consider the mystery you are about to contemplate. Remain for a moment within that scene from the lives of Jesus and Mary.

03

Ask for the grace you need

As you contemplate the mystery, ask for the grace you need now. It may be a virtue in which you hope to grow, or a habit or difficulty you long to overcome.

04

Do not give up when distractions come

Do not be discouraged when other thoughts arise during prayer. Once you notice that your mind has wandered, quietly return to the mystery and the prayer.

05

Do not hurry to finish

How quickly you finish is not what matters. Attend to each phrase of the Our Father and Hail Mary, and pray slowly without rushing.

06

Begin again, even on difficult days

Some days prayer feels peaceful, and other days it does not. Even when it is hard to focus, there is value in holding on to the Rosary and returning to prayer.

WITH MARY

With Mary is not an app for finishing the Rosary quickly. It was created to help you contemplate the mysteries of Jesus and Mary slowly and continue without losing the flow of prayer.
