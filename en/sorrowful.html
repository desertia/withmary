✝️ Sorrowful Mysteries

SORROWFUL MYSTERIES

The Sorrowful Mysteries contemplate the Passion and death on the Cross that Jesus endured for us.
They invite us to behold the Lord’s love and sacrifice and to offer our own suffering in Christ.

FIRST MYSTERY

The Agony in the Garden

Luke 22:39–46

We contemplate Jesus in the deep anguish of Gethsemane, remaining obedient to the will of the Father.

SECOND MYSTERY

The Scourging at the Pillar

John 19:1

We contemplate Jesus silently accepting cruel scourging and suffering for our salvation.

THIRD MYSTERY

The Crowning with Thorns

Matthew 27:27–31

We contemplate the humility and patience of Jesus, who did not withdraw his love amid mockery and humiliation.

FOURTH MYSTERY

The Carrying of the Cross

Luke 23:26–32

We contemplate Jesus carrying the Cross toward Golgotha and walking the path of salvation to the end.

FIFTH MYSTERY

The Crucifixion and Death

Luke 23:33–46

We contemplate the love of Jesus, who gives himself completely on the Cross for our salvation.
