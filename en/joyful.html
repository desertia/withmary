🌹 Joyful Mysteries

JOYFUL MYSTERIES

The Joyful Mysteries contemplate God’s love revealed in the birth and childhood of Jesus.
Through Mary’s faith and obedience, they lead us into the joy of salvation revealed in these five mysteries.

FIRST MYSTERY

The Annunciation

Luke 1:38

We contemplate Mary’s faith and obedience as she freely accepts the will of the Lord.

SECOND MYSTERY

The Visitation

Luke 1:39–45

We contemplate Mary, carrying Jesus within her, visiting Elizabeth and serving her with love.

THIRD MYSTERY

The Nativity

Luke 2:1–20

We contemplate Jesus being born in a humble manger in Bethlehem and bringing the joy of salvation to the world.

FOURTH MYSTERY

The Presentation in the Temple

Luke 2:22–35

We contemplate Mary and Saint Joseph presenting the child Jesus in the Temple in obedience to God’s will.

FIFTH MYSTERY

The Finding of Jesus in the Temple

Luke 2:41–52

We contemplate Mary and Saint Joseph finding Jesus in the Temple after searching for him for three days.
