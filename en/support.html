Frequently Asked Questions

Why was With Mary created?

With Mary was created by Remigius, a Catholic from South Korea, after experiencing moments while traveling when a rosary was not at hand. The Rosary is traditionally prayed with rosary beads. With Mary is not meant to replace them, but to help you continue your prayer when your rosary is not with you.

What is With Mary?

With Mary is an iPhone and Apple Watch app that helps you pray the Rosary with guided prayer steps and texts.

Can I stop and continue my Rosary later?

Yes. You can pause your prayer and continue it later.

Can I pray the Rosary on Apple Watch?

Yes. With Mary supports Apple Watch. You can begin on your iPhone or Apple Watch and continue your Rosary prayer on the other device.

Is With Mary free?

Yes. With Mary is free to download and use.

Can I use With Mary without an internet connection?

Basic Rosary prayers and core prayer features can be used without an internet connection.

Is With Mary suitable for someone new to the Rosary?

Yes. Guided prayer steps and texts make it easy to follow even if you are new to the Rosary.

Which prayers are supported?

With Mary includes the Joyful, Luminous, Sorrowful, and Glorious Mysteries, along with a variety of Catholic prayers.

Can I save my prayer history?

Prayer history and the liturgical calendar help you maintain a consistent prayer life.

Which devices are supported?

With Mary is available for iPhone and Apple Watch and can be downloaded from the App Store.

When is my prayer history saved?

Your prayer progress and completion records are added to your account through the saving features provided by the app.

Can I view my history after changing devices?

If you sign in with the same account, you can view the records stored on the server again.

The app is not working properly. What should I do?

Fully close the app, open it again, and make sure you are using the latest version.

What does the Spanish inscription on the rosary bead mean?

MARIA NUESTRA SRA. DE GUADALUPE · EMPERATRIZ DE AMERICA · RUEGA POR NOSOTROS

It means: “Mary, Our Lady of Guadalupe, Empress of the Americas, pray for us.”
