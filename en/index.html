OUR STORY

For the moments when your rosary is not with you.

With Mary was created by Remigius, a Catholic developer from South Korea.

The idea began while traveling, when he wanted to pray the Rosary in a beautiful place but did not have his rosary with him.

The Rosary is most naturally prayed with rosary beads. With Mary is not meant to replace them—it is here to help you keep praying when they are not close at hand.

Wherever you are, pray with Mary.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is With Mary?

With Mary is an iPhone and Apple Watch app that guides you through the Rosary with prayer steps and traditional prayer texts.

Can I pause the Rosary and continue later?

Yes. You can pause your prayer and continue later from where you left off.

Can I pray the Rosary on Apple Watch?

Yes. Start on your iPhone or Apple Watch, then continue your Rosary on the other device.

Is With Mary free?

Yes. With Mary is free to download and use.

What does the Spanish inscription on the rosary bead mean?

MARIA NUESTRA SRA. DE GUADALUPE · EMPERATRIZ DE AMERICA · RUEGA POR NOSOTROS

It means: “Mary, Our Lady of Guadalupe, Empress of the Americas, pray for us.”
